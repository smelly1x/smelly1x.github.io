We have made it to 100 days. The happiest 100 days I have ever experienced. Fiaza, you have changed my life. You have consumed my life so quickly that I seem to forget that, not that long ago, I detested the idea of sharing a life with someone, sharing space and memories, and loving someone. I had already accepted the fact that I was never going to have a partner. My life was meant to be condemned to solitude, and then you happened.

The greatest decision I’ve ever made was saying yes. On the 1st of May, for some odd reason, I agreed to something I had never agreed to before. Completely randomly, with no expectations in the world, I just said yes. YOU are the greatest thing that has ever happened to me.

I remember, for years, I would pray and consciously make the decision to NOT mention marriage to Allah. Not out of contempt, but because for the longest time I didn’t think I could ever be loved. I feared not being good enough for someone. I had never felt love, so how could I love someone else? I didn’t love myself enough to even begin imagining loving someone else. And yet here we are, moments away from spending the rest of our lives together, forever.

I tell you all the time how much I love you, but I fear you don’t quite understand it. There aren’t enough words in the dictionary to describe what love is. But what I can say is that you are the peace in my life, the quiet I return to every night when all is said and done, the comfort I seek in troubled times, the love I wish I could surround myself with for all eternity. When I’m talking to you, nothing else matters. There isn’t a thing in the world that could take my attention away from you. You remind me that the world isn’t that bad after all.

I always wondered: if I ever found someone I wanted to spend the rest of my life with, what would they be like? Would I ever be compatible with anyone? Was I even capable of being loved? I will forever be grateful for the change that has happened since you came into my life. I have never felt more alive. It feels like an empty space I didn’t even know existed has been filled. Everything with you just feels so perfect. There’s not a day that goes by where YOU aren’t the best part of my day. I cherish every conversation, every phone call, every voice note, every hug, every kiss, every time we hold hands, every time we visit each other as if it’s the last time. Why? Because that is how much I value what we have. THAT is how much I love you.

The beautiful thing about what we have is that neither of us were ready. It wasn’t something we were looking for; it wasn’t something we even thought about. And yet it stands proud as the greatest thing we’ve ever done. It wasn’t in our plans, it wasn’t even something we wanted, yet before even meeting each other we did something that would change the trajectory of our lives for the better. Your greatest gift to me will always be exactly that.

100 days doesn’t seem like a long time on paper, but we didn’t need 100 days to say I love you, we only needed 63. It only took us 77 days to tell our families. It took us 27 days to call each other husband and wife. But none of that changes the fact that I have treated you with the love and respect I would if you were my wife. In my mind, you already are, which is why from the very first day I treated you that way.

Even just writing all of this has made me so incredibly emotional. Reflecting on the past three months has made me realise how deeply I love you. Often, love is categorised as the actions people do for you, or the way someone makes you feel. Not for me. I love everything about you. Rereading some of our older chats is one of my favourite things to do. From the very beginning, we knew what our intentions were with this, and it’s clear that we have BOTH treated each other with the dignity and respect we would want as husband and wife. We knew very early on that this is what we want, and that alone is telling enough that everything we have done, from the very first day, was as if we are already married.

It feels as though I have known you my entire life. The manner in which you entered my life just felt right. It was truly so perfect. The timing couldn’t have been better for the both of us. I was always told that you’ll find the one when you least expect it, and that is exactly what happened. I didn’t think I was ready. I didn’t know that this was something I wanted. You came into my life at a time when I had no idea what was going to happen next. I remember how nervous I was seeing you for the first time. Being able to hold you, the source of my happiness, being able to physically touch that source, was a surreal feeling. I have cherished every moment with you. Sometimes I have to pinch myself to realise that YES, this really is my life right now. I’m not dreaming.

Fiaza, I want to spend the rest of my life with you. I have already spent 100 days loving you like a husband would. So let this be the promise. I will keep showing up. I will keep choosing you. I will keep treating you like my wife long before anyone signs anything, because in my heart it was decided a long time ago. Every day from here is just me proving what I already know.

I promise to be patient with you when you are tired, and gentle with you when you are hurting. I promise that you will never have to wonder where you stand with me. I promise to protect your peace the way you have protected mine. And I promise that whatever comes, you will not face it on your own.

Fiaza, I love you so much.
